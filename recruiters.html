Honest answers

The questions every recruiter asks before signing up.

If yours isn't here, email hello@salescard.ai.

Where do the verified numbers come from?

Reps upload eight quarters of stats themselves. Then they invite their former managers, peer reps, or RevOps partners to confirm each quarter via email. Confirmed quarters carry full weight in the score; unverified quarters carry half. You see exactly who confirmed what and when.

How big is the verified pool right now?

We're in early access. We're onboarding the first 1,000 reps and the first 25 recruiters together so the pool is dense at launch. Reach out and we'll show you who's already in your target segment.

What if a verification gets flagged as inaccurate?

The verifier can mark a quarter as wrong. You see the flag immediately on the candidate's card, and so does the candidate. We never quietly hide flagged data — the whole point of the platform is that you see receipts, including bad ones.

Can my hiring managers see verified cards?

Yes. Solo includes 3 free hiring-manager seats; Team includes 10. They get read-only access to candidates you've shortlisted and can sign off on shortlists in the same place you're working.

Is there a per-placement fee or commission share?

No. Pay your subscription, place as many candidates as you want. We don't take any cut of your fees.

How are reps incentivized to join?

It's free forever for reps. Their card is portable — between jobs, on LinkedIn, in DMs to recruiters. We pull the demand side (you), and they show up for the same reason every sales rep wants a verified scoreboard: it travels with them.

What's the refund policy?

60 days, no questions. If you don't put at least three verified candidates in front of your clients during that window, email us and we refund every dollar — annual or monthly, doesn't matter.
